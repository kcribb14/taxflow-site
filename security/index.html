Security and privacy

Your data stays on your device.

TaxFlow is a web page that does all of its work in your browser. There is no TaxFlow server, no account and no database of anyone's trades. This page explains exactly what that means.

How it works

Local-first, step by step.

  1. 1
    The page loadsYour browser downloads TaxFlow’s code from the website host, like any web page. That is the only download it needs.
  2. 2
    You choose your filesThe page reads them from your disk with the browser’s file picker. They are not sent anywhere.
  3. 3
    The engine runs in the tabA background worker in the same tab matches transfers and works out every disposal, then the reports.
  4. 4
    Your project is saved on this deviceIn the browser’s own database, so it is there when you come back on the same browser.

Storage

What is stored, and where.

Everything below stays on the device you use. Another browser or computer starts empty unless you move a backup across.

Network

What can leave your device.

By default: nothing about you

The website host sees the request for each page and file, as every host does, including your IP address and browser. It never sees your tax data, because the page never sends it.

Price lookup, if you turn it on

TaxFlow asks public price services for a coin’s AUD price on a date: BTC Markets, Kraken, CoinGecko (only if you add a key), Coinbase, Binance, and Frankfurter for currency rates. It sends coin symbols and dates. Never amounts, balances, wallets or files.

Nothing else

No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no third-party fonts or scripts. This website and the app load everything from their own host.

Deleting

How to delete your data.

TaxFlow has no copy of your data, so deleting it on your device deletes it everywhere TaxFlow can reach.

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Under Delete all data, choose Delete all data and confirm. Your files, fixes, manual transactions and settings are removed from this browser.
  3. To also remove cached prices and everything else, clear this site’s data in your browser’s privacy settings.
  4. Delete any exports or backup files you saved, if you no longer need them.

Open the app to do it now.

Your part

What local-first cannot protect against.

Keeping data on your device moves the responsibility for that device to you. In particular:

  • Shared computers. Anyone who uses the same browser profile can open your project. Use your own profile, or delete your data when you finish.
  • Backups and exports are private records. A project backup contains your raw exchange files and any CoinGecko key you saved. Store it like a bank statement.
  • Browser extensions can read the pages you visit. Use a profile without extensions you do not trust.
  • Clearing your browser removes your project for good. Keep a backup and your original exchange files.

Report a security problem

If you find a way TaxFlow could expose someone’s data, open an issue that says a security problem exists, without the details, so they are not public before a fix. See Contact.
