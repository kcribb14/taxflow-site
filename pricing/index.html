Questions

About the beta.

Why is it free?

TaxFlow is in beta. The aim right now is to get the calculations and the experience right with real people and real exchange files, and to hear about everything that breaks.

What does beta mean for me?

Every feature works, and the tax engine is checked against hand-worked cases before each change. Screens and wording may still change, and some layouts are marked unconfirmed until checked against a real file. Check your figures before you lodge.

Will it stay free?

Pricing after the beta has not been set. Your data is on your device and the exports are plain CSV and JSON files, so you are never locked in either way.

Is there a limit on transactions?

No. Everything runs on your device, so the only limit is what your browser can handle.
