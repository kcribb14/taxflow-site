Which file to download from each exchange, and what TaxFlow does with it.

  • Export your CoinSpot historyBuilt-in readerDownload CoinSpot's Buys/Sells CSV and Sends/Receives CSV, tell TaxFlow the export time zone, and know what is not in either file.
  • Export your Swyftx historyBuilt-in readerDownload the Swyftx transaction report as CSV for your whole history, set the export time zone, and see which rows TaxFlow reads.
  • Export your BTC Markets historyBuilt-in readerDownload BTC Markets' Transaction History CSV (trades, deposits and withdrawals) and, if you like, the Trade History CSV.
  • Export your Independent Reserve historyMapper presetIndependent Reserve has no built-in importer yet. Download the Transaction History CSV per currency and map it with the preset.
  • Export your CoinJar historyMapper presetRequest CoinJar's Purchases and Sales report, name its date column (or use the mapper preset), and handle deposits and withdrawals.
  • Export your Digital Surge historyColumn mapperDigital Surge exports three CSVs. Map them with the column mapper and save the mapping for next year.
  • Export your Kraken historyBuilt-in readerCreate a Ledgers export (not Trades) as CSV from Kraken's Documents page, with all fields and your full date range.
  • Export your Binance historyBuilt-in readerDownload Binance's Transaction History statement for every balance change, in six-month chunks if needed, plus deposits and withdrawals.
  • Export your Coinbase historyBuilt-in readerGenerate Coinbase's all-time transaction history CSV, plus legacy Coinbase Pro files if you used Pro, and what TaxFlow reads in them.
  • Export your Crypto.com App historyBuilt-in readerExport the Crypto.com App's crypto wallet transaction history as CSV, in three-year chunks, and download it without opening it on your phone.
  • Export your OKX historyBuilt-in readerDownload OKX trading history and funding history as CSV, note the time zone you choose at export, and add both to TaxFlow.
  • Export your KuCoin historyBuilt-in readerExport KuCoin's trade, deposit and withdrawal history one year at a time, drop the zip in whole, and know which files TaxFlow reads.
  • Export your Bybit historyColumn mapperBybit has no built-in importer yet. Export the Transaction Log and spot trade history, then map them with the column mapper.
  • Any other exchange: the column mapperColumn mapperImport any CSV, Excel sheet or zip by telling TaxFlow what each column means, check a dry run, and save the mapping to reuse.
