Carried-forward capital losses

How net capital losses carry forward, how to enter losses from before your first imported year, and the separate rule for collectable losses.

A capital loss you cannot use this year is not wasted. It becomes a net capital loss that carries forward to later years with no time limit, and is applied against future capital gains. It cannot be taken off your salary or other income. Your return reports it at label V, "Net capital losses carried forward to later income years".

How TaxFlow carries losses

TaxFlow works out every year from the start of your history, in order. Each year:

  1. Current year capital losses come off current year gains, non-discount gains first.
  2. Losses carried in from earlier years come off what is left, non-discount gains first.
  3. The CGT discount applies only to discount gains that remain after both.
  4. Whatever is left unused is label V, and becomes next year's carried-in losses.

You cannot choose to save a loss for a later year: losses must be applied against gains as soon as there are gains. A year with no disposals at all still carries its losses forward.

An example

2023-24: you sell ETH at a $4,000 loss and have no gains. Label H is $0, A is $0, and V is $4,000.

2024-25: you sell BTC held for two years at a $10,000 gain. H is $10,000. The $4,000 carried in comes off it, leaving $6,000. Half of that is the discount ($3,000). A is $3,000, V is $0.

Losses from before your first imported year

If you had capital losses before the earliest history in your project (from shares, property or crypto you have not imported), TaxFlow needs to know.

  1. Find label V on your most recent return before your first imported year. A copy of that return, or your agent, can confirm it.
  2. Enter it in Settings as Net capital losses carried in (AUD).
  3. Check Reports: the assumptions list the amount, and the CGT schedule shows Prior year net capital losses applied.

Check

carried-forward losses are shared across all your CGT assets, not just crypto. If you also sold shares this year, the losses (and the gains) belong on one return together. TaxFlow only sees crypto, so your agent or myTax combines them. Tell your agent if TaxFlow applied losses that you also expect to use against share gains.

Collectable losses

Losses on collectables (in crypto, typically NFTs that are artwork or similar) can only be used against collectable gains. TaxFlow keeps them separate:

  • Current and carried-in collectable losses come off collectable gains first.
  • Unused collectable losses carry forward separately (CGT schedule label 3A), while other losses are 3B. Together they make V.
  • Enter collectable losses from before your first imported year in Settings as Collectable losses carried in (AUD).

See Collectables and NFTs.

Losses that do not count

  • Personal use assets: losses are disregarded. See Personal use assets.
  • Disposals while you were a foreign resident (other than on taxable Australian property) are disregarded, gains and losses alike.
  • Trading stock: a trader's losses are business losses, not capital losses. They do not appear at label V.

Companies and SMSFs

The same order applies. Companies get no discount; SMSFs get one third. Companies have extra tests for using prior-year losses (continuity of ownership or the business continuity test) that TaxFlow does not check: your agent will.
