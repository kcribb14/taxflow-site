Export your Binance history

Download Binance's Transaction History statement for every balance change, in six-month chunks if needed, plus deposits and withdrawals.

Open the app

Binance (including Binance Australia, which uses the same export screens) has several exports. The one to get is the Transaction History statement, because it records every balance change: spot trades, Convert, card buys, small-balance conversions to BNB, staking and Earn rewards, fees paid in BNB, deposits and withdrawals. The trade-only exports miss most of those.

Which export to use

Export TaxFlow When to use it
Transaction History (statement) Read Always, first choice
Trade History (CSV or XLSX, three generations) Read Only for periods where you cannot get the statement
Deposit and withdrawal history (crypto and fiat) Read Only if not using the statement
Convert history Not read The statement already contains every Convert
Order History Refused It has no fee column
Futures Refused Out of scope

Use one route for a given period. Adding the statement and the Trade History for the same months counts those trades twice.

How to get the Transaction History statement

  1. Sign in on the Binance website (the app's exports are more limited).
  2. Go to Assets, then Asset History.
  3. Hover over the export icon and choose Export Transaction Records.
  4. Choose the time range (a preset, or a custom range), the account and coins (all), and select Generate.
  5. Binance notifies you when it is ready. Come back to the same pop-up and select Download. Download within 7 days, or it expires.

Binance limits how many statements you can generate per month (it says 15 to 40, depending on the account) and how much fits in one. Request your history in chunks (for example six months at a time, or per year) from your first deposit to today, and add every file.

Note

an app export holds at most 10,000 rows. If a file has exactly that many, TaxFlow warns POSSIBLY_TRUNCATED: export the missing period too.

Add it to TaxFlow

  1. Open Import and drop in every file (CSV, XLSX or zip). TaxFlow recognises each by its header, for example statement-v2 or trades-gen2.
  2. Use the wallet name Binance for all of them.
  3. No time zone question for the statement: its times are UTC. The newest XLSX Trade History states its own offset in a Period(UTC...) cell, which TaxFlow reads.
  4. For older deposit or withdrawal files with no direction column, set Deposit or withdrawal file on the file card. TaxFlow guesses from the file name and warns you to confirm.

What TaxFlow does with the statement

The statement has no trade id. The legs of one trade are the rows in the same second (sometimes straddling into the next second), and TaxFlow groups them: exactly one asset out and one asset in, plus fee rows. If several unrelated trades happened in the same second and cannot be told apart, the group is listed as unsupported rather than split by guesswork.

  • Trades and conversions become trades. Fees in the received asset are netted; fees in another asset (often BNB) are added on top and are themselves a small disposal of that BNB.
  • Interest, staking, launchpool and referral rows become income.
  • Airdrops and distributions become airdrops (not income, cost base at market value, under draft TR 2026/D1; see Airdrops).
  • Binance Card Spending is a disposal at market value.
  • Moves between your own Binance balances (Spot to Funding, Earn subscriptions and redemptions) are ignored with the operation named.
  • Futures, margin, loans and unknown operations are listed as unsupported.
  • User_ID is personal data. It is blanked when the file is read and never stored in results.

Values you will need to add

Most Binance trades are against USDT or other crypto, not AUD, and income rows carry no price. Expect many missing AUD values. Turn on Look up missing prices online in Settings and accept suggestions in Review. Note that USDT is a crypto asset, not cash: swapping into USDT is a disposal.

Gotchas

  • Whether a withdrawal's amount includes its fee is not documented by Binance. TaxFlow reads the amount as what was sent, with the fee alongside. If your withdrawals do not match the deposits in your wallet, compare the two amounts.
  • Binance Australia's terms name an Australian company, though some services may come from affiliates. If you are registered for GST, confirm where your account sits in Settings. See BAS and GST for crypto.
