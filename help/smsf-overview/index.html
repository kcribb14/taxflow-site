SMSF crypto in TaxFlow: an overview

Set up a self-managed super fund project, how the one-third CGT discount applies, and which reports and exports an SMSF gets.

A self-managed super fund (SMSF) is its own taxpayer, so its crypto belongs in its own TaxFlow project, separate from any member's personal crypto. Set the taxpayer to SMSF and TaxFlow applies the fund's CGT rules and offers the hand-off files for BGL Simple Fund 360 and Class Super.

Set up the fund's project

  1. Create a new project from the project menu in the header, named after the fund. Or choose SMSF on the Welcome screen.
  2. In Settings, set Taxpayer to SMSF.
  3. Import only the fund's exchange accounts and wallets: accounts opened in the fund's name, holding the fund's assets. Personal accounts stay in the members' own projects.
  4. Set the time zone to where the fund's records are kept. The 30 June cut-off uses it.

How the tax rules differ for a fund

Rule Individual SMSF (complying fund)
CGT discount on assets held 12 months or more 50% One third (33⅓%)
Treatment Investor or trader Always capital account (TaxFlow does not offer trader mode for an SMSF)
Residency settings Available Not used
Tax rate in the estimate Individual rates 15%
PAYG instalment income Excludes capital gains Includes the period's net capital gain

Everything else works the same way: the 12-month test (held for at least 12 months, not counting the days of acquisition and disposal), losses applied before the discount and non-discount gains first, staking and interest as income at market value, and parcel methods. The fund's net capital gain and carried-forward losses go on the SMSF annual return rather than myTax; your administrator or accountant prepares it.

Note

TaxFlow gives general information and calculations, not tax or super advice. Whether the fund may hold crypto at all depends on its trust deed and investment strategy, and on the super law rules (such as the sole purpose test and keeping fund assets separate from members' assets). Those are questions for the fund's adviser and auditor, not for software.

What a fund gets in TaxFlow

  • Reports: the capital gains figures with the one-third discount, the CGT schedule when needed, income, checks and assumptions, and the tax agent pack.
  • SMSF files (on Reports, shown only when the taxpayer is SMSF):
    • bgl-transactions.xml: a BGL File Import XML built from BGL's official template. See BGL Simple Fund 360 export.
    • bgl-transactions.csv and bgl-prices.csv: compact worksheets for BGL.
    • class-portfolio.csv, class-market-prices.csv and class-checklist.txt: for Class Super's Excel Transaction Loader. See Class Super export.
    • rounding-residual-bgl.csv and rounding-residual-class.csv: units lost to the decimal places each product accepts.
  • Holdings: parcels held at 30 June with cost base, and the 30 June market values the price files and the auditor need.

30 June market values

A fund reports its assets at market value each year. Enter each coin's AUD price on 30 June in Holdings (or accept online price suggestions), and keep where each price came from. Both price files use these values; a coin with no price gets an empty price and a note.

Before you export

The SMSF transaction files refuse to build while the year has a missing AUD value, because a placeholder is not a market value and must not become an accounting entry. Clear Review for the year first. Deliberate zero-cost items (chain splits, initial allocations) are fine.
