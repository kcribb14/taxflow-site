Export your KuCoin history

Export KuCoin's trade, deposit and withdrawal history one year at a time, drop the zip in whole, and know which files TaxFlow reads.

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

KuCoin exports your history as a set of files, often a zip containing many CSVs, each covering a year or less. TaxFlow reads the spot order (fill) files and the deposit and withdrawal files inside it. You can drop the whole zip in; TaxFlow opens it and adds each file it recognises as a separate entry.

How to get it

  1. Sign in to KuCoin on the website (exports are not available in the app).
  2. Go to Orders (the Order Center) and select Export.
  3. Choose the market or account (all), the time range and the time zone, and include sub-accounts if you use them.
  4. Submit. KuCoin notifies you by SMS, email or on-site message when the export is ready.
  5. Go to Orders, then Export (or Export Data), then Recent Exports, and select Download. Links stay valid for 30 days.

Each export can cover at most one year, and KuCoin allows a limited number of exports per month (20 at the time of writing). Repeat for each year since you opened your account. Choose CSV or Excel; TaxFlow reads both.

Note

KuCoin data before mid-2021 may need a request to KuCoin support. If your account is older, check that your earliest buys are in the files.

Add it to TaxFlow

  1. Open Import and drop in the zip (or the CSVs). Each recognised file appears as its own entry: spot-orders-v1, spot-orders-v2, deposits or withdrawals.
  2. Use the wallet name KuCoin for all of them.
  3. Untick any entry you do not want, then Add selected files.

Recent files write the time zone into the column header, such as Filled Time(UTC+10:00), so there is no question to answer. Older files without a zone in the header ask for the Export time zone.

What TaxFlow does with it

Spot orders. Each row is one filled order: Symbol is the pair (for example BTC-USDT), Filled Amount the coin quantity and Filled Volume the quote quantity.

  • A buy gives up the quote and receives the coin; a sell does the reverse.
  • A fee in the asset you received is taken off it. A fee in another asset (often KCS) is added on top and is itself a small disposal of that KCS.
  • Orders that were not completed, or filled nothing, are ignored.

Deposits and withdrawals become one-sided transfers, paired with your other wallets.

Personal data. The UID column is blanked when the file is read and never stored in results. Empty exports contain a line "No matching records found."; it is ignored.

Files TaxFlow does not read

The zip also holds files for Convert, staking and Earn, margin, futures, account ledgers and fiat or P2P orders. They show as entries TaxFlow could not identify. Options:

  • Convert and staking: use the column mapper on them, or add the trades and rewards by hand in Transactions. Without them, coins you converted or earned will show as sales with no purchase on file.
  • Margin and futures: outside TaxFlow's scope.
  • Fiat buys (card or P2P for AUD): map them, or add them by hand as purchases.

Values you will need to add

Almost all KuCoin pairs are against USDT, BTC or KCS, so most trades have no AUD value and are flagged in Review. The Filled Volume (USDT) column is a USD figure, not AUD, and is not used as a value. Turn on Look up missing prices online in Settings to get suggestions, or bring a price file.

Gotchas

  • Do not mix time zones. If you download some years in one zone and others in another, TaxFlow reads each file's own header, so that is fine, but older files without a header zone all take the zone you enter for them.
  • Overlapping years. Make sure the year ranges you export do not overlap, or the overlapping trades will be counted twice.
