The tax estimate and what it leaves out

What TaxFlow's tax estimate includes, the rates it uses for 2024-25 to 2026-27, and the levies, offsets and rules it does not model.

The Tax estimate screen gives a rough idea of what you will owe, or get back, once your crypto is included. It is a planning figure, not your assessment. The ATO works out your actual tax from your whole return.

What you enter

Field What to put in
Residency for the whole year Resident or foreign resident. Part-year residency is not worked out here.
Other taxable income Salary, interest, dividends and so on, before the separate deductions below; leave out crypto already included automatically
Other deductions Eligible deductions you have checked and have not already subtracted or included in crypto figures
Tax already withheld PAYG withheld by your employer or payer (from your income statement)
PAYG instalments paid Instalments you paid during the year
Exempt from the Medicare levy Tick only if you have an exemption
Company tax rate Companies only: 25% or 30%

Your entries are saved with your project.

For resident individuals, Use individual records is an alternative to totals. Add wage statements, your share of Australian bank interest and eligible deductions, with a source reference and explicit withholding. Check each record and mark it reviewed. Blank or unreviewed records block a numeric record-based estimate; enter zero only when known. Switching methods keeps the other entries, but only the selected method is counted. The printable income worksheet covers these records, not your whole return.

Optional Medicare family facts

Open Medicare family reduction if you have checked that you fit its circumstances. This module covers 2025–26 only, for adult couples married or de facto together on 30 June, both full-year Australian residents using ordinary rates, with no SAPTO, Medicare exemptions or special super/lump-sum income adjustments. Single parents, other relationships and other circumstances need a separate check.

Enter your partner's checked Medicare taxable income in whole dollars, the number of children whose eligibility for this reduction you have checked, and a source reference. Blank means unknown; use zero only when confirmed. The child rules include maintenance, age/student and income tests; this is not simply your household size or the Medicare levy surcharge dependant count. TaxFlow calculates from the checked facts and does not decide eligibility. Mark the facts reviewed; editing clears review. Excluding the module retains the facts and returns to the limited single-person calculation.

Partner income is used only in the Medicare family test. It is never added to your own taxable income, withholding or HELP repayment income. TaxFlow applies the statutory family reduction and spouse allocation, including an allocation that exceeds one partner's levy. It keeps the actual family-relief cause separate from displayed cents. See the Medicare Levy Act, sections 3, 6, 7 and 8. Amounts are estimates, not verified ATO assessment rounding.

Optional HELP repayment

Open Study loan (HELP) to include a checked compulsory repayment. This module covers 2025–26 only, for a full-year Australian resident adult with HELP debt only and no First Home Super Saver released amount or Medicare exemption. Without checked family facts it covers the ordinary single-person case with no Medicare family reduction. With the checked couple facts above, TaxFlow also tests the section 8 family relief. You must confirm the covered circumstances; TaxFlow does not determine eligibility. Other circumstances and years need a separate check.

Enter your repayable HELP debt, extra repayment income and a source reference, then mark the facts reviewed. Use the relevant accumulated debt at the 1 June immediately before assessment, less payments and amounts already assessed against it. Extra repayment income combines reportable fringe benefits, total net investment losses, reportable super contributions and eligible exempt foreign employment income. Blank means unknown; No repayment-income additions explicitly records zero. Editing facts clears review. Excluding HELP retains them and returns the limited estimate.

If the checked Medicare family calculation establishes section 8 relief, HESA 154-1(2) excludes a compulsory HELP repayment. This does not write off your loan or create a tax credit. Changing or excluding family facts clears HELP scope confirmation and review, so check HELP again before including it. A displayed zero Medicare levy or zero family reduction alone cannot establish this relief.

Otherwise the repayment uses taxable income plus these additions: 15% above $67,000 up to $125,000, then 17% above $125,000, capped at 10% of repayment income and your repayable debt. The result names HELP separately and includes it in the balance. Existing employer withholding counts once in the usual withheld total; do not add it again as a HELP credit. Voluntary repayments reduce debt rather than creating another tax credit. Figures are estimated cents, not an ATO debt assessment. See the Higher Education Support Act, sections 154-1 to 154-20.

HELP and family facts stay local and in your backups. HELP-only facts require an app that can read backup/project format3; family facts require format4. Older versions refuse them safely. Keep a private backup and its password before moving devices.

What it adds from your crypto

  • Your net capital gain (label A).
  • Crypto income (staking, interest, rewards and so on), except income received while a foreign or temporary resident.
  • Foreign exchange gains less losses.
  • For a trader, the net trading income instead (which already includes crypto income received in the business).

Taxable income is that total plus your other income, less your other deductions, rounded down to whole dollars and never below zero.

What it calculates

  • Income tax at the individual rates for the year. For 2024-25 and 2025-26: nil to $18,200, 16% to $45,000, 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000 and 45% above. For 2026-27 the 16% rate becomes 15%. Foreign residents use the foreign resident rates (30% from the first dollar to $135,000, then 37% and 45%).
  • Low income tax offset for residents: up to $700, phasing out completely by $66,667. It can reduce tax to zero but not below.
  • Medicare levy for residents: 2% of taxable income, with the low-income reduction for singles (no levy at or below the lower threshold, then a shaded-in rate). Foreign residents and anyone you mark exempt pay none.
  • SMSF: 15% of taxable income, for a complying fund with no non-arm's length income.
  • Company: 25% or 30%.
  • Balance: total tax less tax withheld and instalments paid. Positive means you may owe; negative means a refund.

The Rates used panel names the table, and notes anything assumed.

Years covered

Estimates cover 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27. Pick one of those in the year selector at the top. Where the ATO had not yet published a 2026-27 figure when TaxFlow was built, it uses the 2025-26 one and says so: the foreign resident rates and the Medicare levy low-income thresholds are in that position.

What it leaves out

Note

this is general information and a rough calculation, not tax advice. It leaves out a lot, deliberately.

  • Medicare levy surcharge for people without private hospital cover.
  • Medicare exemptions, single-parent and other family cases outside the checked couple scope, and the seniors and pensioners offset. Without checked couple facts it uses the ordinary singles thresholds.
  • Offsets other than the low income tax offset, such as private health insurance rebates, franking credits and foreign income tax offsets.
  • Study and training loan repayments, except explicitly included HELP within the checked 2025–26 circumstances above.
  • Part-year residency and the tax-free threshold apportionment that goes with it.
  • Tax losses. If your total comes out negative, the estimate floors it at zero and notes the loss; it does not carry it forward. Business losses may also be deferred under the non-commercial loss rules.
  • Capital gains from outside crypto, such as shares or property, unless you include them yourself in other taxable income.
  • CGT arrangements from 1 July 2027 are not implemented. The estimate supports 2024–25 to 2026–27 only. Treasury describes changes for gains accruing from 1 July 2027; consult the current Treasury reform guidance and your agent for that period. Holdings can retain valuation records, but an entered value does not make a future-year estimate supported.

Use the estimate to set money aside, not to lodge. Your notice of assessment is the real figure.
