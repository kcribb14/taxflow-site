Delete your data

How to remove a project, everything TaxFlow stored in your browser, and the price cache, and what is left anywhere else (nothing).

Updated 18 October 2026

  2. Delete one project
  3. Delete everything in this browser

  6. On a shared or public computer

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

Everything TaxFlow knows about you is on your device. There is no account to close and no server copy to request deletion of. Deleting is therefore entirely in your hands, and it cannot be undone by anyone, including us.

What TaxFlow stores, and where

What Where Contains
Your projects Browser storage (IndexedDB) for the app's address Your imported files' contents, fixes, manual transactions, settings, saved column mappings, 30 June and residency market values
Provider credentials Separate browser-local record, protected when the optional vault is enabled Any CoinGecko key; omitted from new backups and reports
Price cache Browser storage Prices fetched online (coin, date, AUD price, source), only if you turned on online lookup
Small preferences Browser local storage Conveniences such as the website's light or dark theme choice
Files you downloaded Your downloads folder Backups (.taxflow.json), tax agent packs, CSVs

Nothing is sent to TaxFlow. If you turned on online price lookup, the price providers you chose received coin symbols and dates (no transaction quantities or wallets; providers also receive normal connection metadata such as your IP address and any required provider key) and handle their own logs under their own policies.

Delete one project

Open the project menu in the app's header, choose the project, and delete it. The app asks you to confirm. Other projects in the same browser are not touched.

Delete everything in this browser

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Under Delete all data, choose Delete all data.
  3. Confirm with Yes, delete everything, or back out with Keep my data.

This removes your files, fixes, manual transactions and settings from this browser. TaxFlow has no copy anywhere else.

Check

export a backup first if there is any chance you will need these figures again. The ATO expects you to keep records for at least five years after the relevant disposal, with longer retention sometimes needed for carried-forward losses; follow the linked record-keeping guidance, and the tax agent pack is a good record of how your figures were worked out.

Delete it at the browser level too

To be thorough, also clear the site data for the app's address in your browser settings (in most browsers: Settings, Privacy, Site data or Cookies and site data, then search for the address). That also removes local protection, cache, preferences and non-identifying coordination metadata. Using the browser's "Clear browsing data" for all sites does the same, along with everything else.

Do not forget the files you downloaded

Backups, agent packs and CSVs you downloaded are ordinary files on your computer. Delete them from your downloads folder and empty the bin if you no longer want them. If you uploaded any of them to a cloud drive or emailed them to an agent, those copies are outside TaxFlow and need deleting there.

On a shared or public computer

Use a private window, export a backup to your own storage before you close it, and close the window when you finish. Private windows usually discard browser storage when closed, but do not rely on that alone: delete all data from Settings first.
