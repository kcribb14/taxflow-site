What TaxFlow is, and what it is not

A local-first Australian crypto tax calculator. What it works out, where your data lives, and where a registered tax agent still matters.

Updated 23 October 2026

Open the app

TaxFlow turns the exports from your crypto exchanges and wallets into the figures an Australian tax return asks for. You drop in your files, fix what it flags, and leave with the amounts to type into myTax, plus a pack of files for your tax agent. It runs entirely in your browser: your files are read on your device, the calculation runs on your device, and your project is saved on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is no "us" server to upload to.

What it works out

  • Capital gains for myTax item 18. Total current year capital gains (label H), net capital gain (label A) and net capital losses carried forward (label V), with the CGT schedule figures when your gains or losses are over $10,000. See myTax item 18, step by step.
  • Other income. Staking rewards, interest, mining and other crypto income, grouped by type with a description to use. See Other income.
  • Business figures. Quarterly or monthly BAS labels for GST-registered businesses, PAYG instalment income, and a trading-stock working if you trade crypto as a business. See BAS and GST for crypto.
  • SMSF hand-offs. A BGL Simple Fund 360 XML file and worksheets, plus Class Super loader files. See SMSF overview.
  • A tax estimate. A rough idea of what you will owe or get back for 2024-25 to 2026-27. See The tax estimate.
  • Records. Every transaction, every parcel matched to every sale, and a "keep until" date for each record.

It handles individuals, SMSFs and companies; investors and traders; Australian, foreign and temporary residents (including the change between them); collectables and personal use assets; foreign currency; airdrops, staking, swaps and network fees.

How it works, in three steps

  1. Import. Add CSV, Excel or zip exports. TaxFlow recognises 11 sources by their column headers and has a column mapper for anything else.
  2. Review. Anything it cannot work out for certain is listed: a missing AUD value, a sale with no purchase on file, a deposit with no matching withdrawal. You decide; it never guesses.
  3. Reports. The myTax guide, the checks it ran, the assumptions it used, and downloads for your agent.

What it is not

Note

TaxFlow gives general information and calculations. It is not a registered tax agent and does not give tax advice. Its figures are only as good as the files you import and the answers you give in Review. Check them with a registered tax agent, especially if your affairs are unusual.

  • It does not lodge. You type the figures into myTax yourself, or your agent uses the pack. No one in the Australian crypto tax software market lodges on your behalf.
  • It does not connect to exchanges. There are no API keys or logins. You download files from each exchange and add them. This is slower than a live sync, but it means nobody else holds a key to your accounts.
  • It does not guess. If an export has no AUD value for a crypto-to-crypto swap, TaxFlow flags it rather than assuming $0. If a file has no time zone, it asks. If an exchange type is not understood, the row is listed as unsupported.
  • It is not a portfolio tracker. It works out tax figures from history. It does not show live prices unless you turn on online price lookup, which is off by default.

Where your data lives

Your project is saved in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on this device. If you clear your browser data, or use another browser or computer, your project is not there. Keep a backup: see Back up and move your data. To remove everything, see Delete your data.

If you turn on online price lookup in Settings, coin symbols and dates are sent directly from your browser to the named price providers. Quantities, wallets and anything that identifies you are never sent.
