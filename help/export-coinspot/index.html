Export your CoinSpot history

Download CoinSpot's Buys/Sells CSV and Sends/Receives CSV, tell TaxFlow the export time zone, and know what is not in either file.

Updated 17 October 2026Built-in reader

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

CoinSpot gives your history as two separate CSV files. TaxFlow reads both with its built-in CoinSpot importer. You need both: one has your trades, the other has every coin you sent out or received.

What to download

File What it holds Usual name
Buys/Sells CSV Every buy and sell (instant buy and market orders) orderhistory.csv
Sends/Receives CSV Coin deposits and withdrawals, including sends to other CoinSpot users varies

How to get them

  1. Sign in to CoinSpot in a desktop browser. The website gives the cleanest files; the mobile app also offers them under Order History, then Reports.
  2. Open the My Account menu and choose Order History.
  3. Download Buys/Sells CSV.
  4. Download Sends/Receives CSV.

CoinSpot may email the file to you rather than download it straight away. Both files cover your whole account history, so you do not need to pick a date range. Download fresh copies each year rather than adding only the new year's rows.

Add them to TaxFlow

  1. Open Import and drop both files in.
  2. TaxFlow recognises each by its header (layout buys-sells or sends-receives). Keep the wallet name as CoinSpot for both, so the two files describe the same account.
  3. Set Export time zone to the zone you were in when you downloaded them, for example Australia/Sydney or Australia/Perth. Both files are refused until you do.
  4. Select Add selected files.

Note

CoinSpot's dates look like 29/7/2022 09:12: day first, no seconds and no time zone. TaxFlow never assumes a zone, because a trade at 11 pm on 30 June in one zone is 1 am on 1 July in another, which moves it into a different tax year. Trades within 24 hours of 1 July get a NEAR_YEAR_END warning so you can double-check them.

What TaxFlow does with each row

Buys/Sells:

  • AUD buys. The AUD you paid (Total, including the fee) becomes the cost of the coins. The fee is part of the cost base.
  • AUD sells. The AUD you received after the fee is your proceeds.
  • Coin-quoted sells (for example AAVE sold for LINK) are crypto-to-crypto swaps. TaxFlow uses CoinSpot's Total AUD as the AUD value. Where that column is 0 (tiny dust trades), the swap is left unvalued and flagged as a missing AUD value rather than priced at $0.
  • Coin-quoted buys do not appear in real CoinSpot exports, so if one turns up it is listed as unsupported.
  • Each row is checked against its own arithmetic (amount times rate, plus or minus the fee). A row that does not add up is listed as an import problem, not guessed at.

The first column is spelled Tansaction Date in some CoinSpot files. That typo is CoinSpot's; TaxFlow reads either spelling.

Sends/Receives:

  • Every send and receive becomes a one-sided transfer, which TaxFlow tries to pair with the other side in another wallet you imported. A send's amount includes CoinSpot's network fee; TaxFlow separates the two.
  • The Aud column (CoinSpot's AUD value at the time) is carried along so that, if you later say a receive was income or a purchase, the value is prefilled. It reads undefined when CoinSpot had none.
  • Small automatic receives such as VTHO or ONG (generated by holding VET or NEO) show up as unmatched deposits. Classify them in Review: usually as income at their value when received.

What is not in either file

  • AUD bank deposits and withdrawals. These have no tax effect, so nothing is lost.
  • Staking rewards and other earnings, if CoinSpot paid them without a send/receive row. If you earned rewards on CoinSpot, compare Holdings with CoinSpot's balance (Holdings shows each wallet separately when Parcels is set to "A pool per wallet" in Settings) and add any missing income by hand in Transactions.

Common problems

  • "Sales with no purchase on file" after importing only Buys/Sells: coins you sold arrived by a receive. Import the Sends/Receives CSV, and the wallet they came from.
  • Unmatched sends to a hardware wallet: import that wallet (see Ledger and hardware wallets) or choose "My own wallet I didn't import" in Review.
