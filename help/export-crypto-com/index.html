Export your Crypto.com App history

Export the Crypto.com App's crypto wallet transaction history as CSV, in three-year chunks, and download it without opening it on your phone.

Updated 17 October 2026Built-in reader

On this page

  1. How to get it
  2. Add it to TaxFlow
  3. What TaxFlow does with it
  4. Gotchas

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

TaxFlow reads the Crypto.com App transaction export, a CSV usually named crypto_transactions_record...csv. The Crypto.com Exchange is a separate product with a different export, which TaxFlow does not read yet.

How to get it

  1. In the Crypto.com App, open Accounts.
  2. Tap the History icon (top right), then Export.
  3. For Transaction, choose the crypto wallet. Recent versions call it Token Wallet; older versions say CRYPTO WALLET.
  4. Pick the start and end dates. One report can cover up to three years, so request several if you have used the app for longer.
  5. Tap Export to CSV.
  6. Open Export History and tap Download when the report is ready. Reports stay downloadable for 30 days.

Check

do not open the file on your phone or Mac before importing it. Numbers and some spreadsheet apps can change dates and long numbers when they open and re-save a CSV. Send the downloaded file straight to your computer and drop it into TaxFlow.

Your Cash Wallet (AUD) and Card exports are separate. Cash wallet movements have no tax effect on their own. You do not need them for TaxFlow.

Add it to TaxFlow

  1. Open Import and drop the CSV in. TaxFlow recognises it by its header (app-v2 with a transaction hash column, or the older app-v1).
  2. Keep the wallet name Crypto.com.
  3. No time zone question: the header says the times are UTC.
  4. Select Add file. If you have several date-range files, add them all.

What TaxFlow does with it

Each row has the coin and amount, sometimes a "to" coin and amount, and a Native Amount: the value at the time in your account's display currency. When that currency is AUD, TaxFlow uses it as the AUD value. If your app displayed another currency, those rows need AUD values entered in Review.

Transaction kind What TaxFlow does
crypto_exchange A swap, in one row
crypto_purchase, viban_purchase, recurring_buy_order A purchase with your AUD (or other fiat)
Swap credited and debited pairs, dust conversions Paired by timestamp into one swap
crypto_deposit, crypto_withdrawal One-sided transfers, paired with your other wallets
Earn interest Income (interest)
Staking rewards Income (staking)
Referral, campaign, gift card rewards Income (reward)
crypto_payment, card_top_up A disposal at market value
Earn, lockup and Exchange transfers Ignored: moves between your own balances
Cashback, reimbursements and reversals Listed as unsupported for you to classify

A dust sweep that turns several small coins into one (more than one coin out at the same second) cannot be split fairly between them, so all its rows are listed as unsupported. Add it by hand in Transactions as separate swaps if the amounts matter.

The App sometimes writes the same row twice. Exact repeats (same second, coin, amount and kind) are ignored as duplicates.

Gotchas

  • Card cashback in CRO is not treated automatically because its tax meaning depends on the circumstances (a rebate on a purchase versus a reward). Decide with your agent, then add it as income or as a purchase at its value.
  • Crypto.com in Australia. The App's Australian terms name an Australian company; the Exchange has a different operator. If you are registered for GST, see BAS and GST for crypto.
  • Exchange users: until TaxFlow reads Crypto.com Exchange files, use the column mapper on its spot trade, deposit and withdrawal CSVs.
