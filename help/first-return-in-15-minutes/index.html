Start with your income or crypto records

Choose a tax task, bring records or enter figures, check what is missing and keep a private copy of your work.

Updated 23 October 2026

On this page

  1. Choose where to start
  2. For income records
  3. For crypto records
  4. Keep a private copy

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

Choose the part you need. You can enter figures yourself or use suitable local files. A payslip PDF is optional. TaxFlow prepares figures and supporting work; it does not lodge a return or cover every tax situation.

Choose where to start

The personal guide asks for the facts needed for your selected situation. Other tax tasks at the start, or More during the guide, opens the wider tools:

  • Add payslips or statements: wages, Australian bank interest and deduction records you have checked. You can use a suitable payslip PDF, an income CSV or manual entry. A fresh project goes through setup first so you can check the year.
  • Work out crypto gains: import exchange and wallet history, review the gaps, then prepare Australian crypto figures.
  • GST and business records: open the BAS worksheet. Its registration, record and eligibility limits still apply.
  • Company or super-fund records: open taxpayer settings for crypto working and accountant handoff. Opening the tools does not convert your project into a different taxpayer.
  • Back up or restore work: keep an editable copy or restore one into a separate project.

Technical screens remain under All tools. Australia and another country provides information for Australia–US, Australia–UK and Australia–New Zealand situations. It does not determine residence or calculate foreign returns, treaty relief or foreign tax offsets.

The personal guide supports projects for individual investors in 2024–25, 2025–26 and 2026–27, subject to its situation and year-specific coverage checks. An unsupported or missing context needs an explicit choice; the app must not silently move your facts to another year.

For income records

Open Tax estimate to add statements. The source worksheet and personal guide keep separate inputs and disclose their own calculation limits.

An income CSV produces a preview before you add unreviewed drafts. Check the source, income type, gross amount and withholding on each record. Do not use net bank deposits as gross wages. A missing amount is unknown; enter zero only when your source confirms it.

A supported payslip PDF is read in this browser. Choose the printed gross and withholding for the current pay, check the dates and name the job. The initial reader accepts a specific readable AUD table layout, not every provider or scanned document. Unsupported or unclear files keep manual/CSV entry available. It does not infer withholding, annualise a pay or import YTD figures as separate pays.

Review each record against its source. Payslip collections also need a deliberate confirmation that all pays received for each job and year are included. One reviewed pay does not establish a full year.

If you arrived from a guide income question, use the visible Return to wages or Return to bank interest link after checking the records. At that question, the checked-source offer shows both income and tax withheld, the source references and the guide's existing answers. Accept it only if the records cover all that income for the year without duplicates. Acceptance replaces both answers once; later source edits do not update them automatically. Incomplete or unknown records need checking first. You can always keep typing or choose I don’t know instead.

For crypto records

Gather the full history from every exchange and wallet you used, including earlier purchases needed for this year's disposals. A tidy export does not prove your history is complete.

Use the exchange guides, such as CoinSpot and Binance. Exchange imports use their supported CSV, Excel or zip formats; the optional payslip PDF reader is a separate income tool. If needed, use the column mapper. Check the wallet name, date interpretation and export time zone before adding files.

Open Review for unmatched transfers, missing prices and purchase gaps. Explain individual transfers from their actual records; an unknown map group is not proof that unrelated transfers belong to one wallet. Ambiguous matches need a deliberate candidate choice. Missing prices stay unresolved until you supply or accept a value. Optional online price requests are disclosed separately.

Then open Reports, select the year and read its checks, assumptions and working. The myTax item 18 guide explains the crypto capital-gains handoff; other crypto income covers the separate income working. Reconcile other assets and return items before relying on whole-return figures.

Keep a private copy

A browser's saved data can be cleared. Download an editable backup from Settings and keep it somewhere private. Check that you can restore it; restore creates a separate project. A printable estimate or accountant pack is useful working, but is not a substitute for the editable backup or proof that a return was lodged.

See backups and moving devices and the tax agent pack for the available handoffs. If a figure or scope note does not fit your situation, keep the source evidence and check the missing work before using it in a return.
