Your first return in 15 minutes

From an empty browser to myTax figures: set up, download your exports, import, clear Review and read the myTax guide.

Updated 17 October 2026

On this page

  2. 1. Set up your project (1 minute)
  3. 2. Import (3 minutes)
  4. 3. Review (5 minutes or more)
  5. 4. Read the myTax guide (1 minute)
  6. 5. Keep a copy

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

Fifteen minutes is realistic if you use one or two exchanges and your history is tidy. If you have used many exchanges, DeFi or self-custody wallets, budget longer for Review. The steps are the same either way.

Before you start: gather your files (5 minutes)

Download the full transaction history from every exchange and wallet you have ever used, not just this financial year. TaxFlow needs your purchases from earlier years to work out the cost of what you sold this year, and to know whether you held it for 12 months.

  • Find the guide for each exchange under the Exchanges category of this help centre, for example CoinSpot or Binance.
  • Pick CSV (or Excel) rather than PDF. TaxFlow cannot read PDFs.
  • Choose "all time" or the widest range the exchange allows. If it caps the range, download several files and add them all.
  • Note the time zone you were in when you downloaded files from CoinSpot, Swyftx, CoinJar, OKX and older KuCoin exports. Those files do not say which zone their times are in, and TaxFlow will ask.

1. Set up your project (1 minute)

When you open the app for the first time, the welcome screen (Welcome) asks who the return is for (individual, SMSF, company or trader), the tax year, and whether you are an Australian resident. These answers go into Settings, where you can change them later. If you are not sure, keep the defaults: individual, investor, Australian resident, FIFO.

2. Import (3 minutes)

Open Import and drop all your files in at once. For each file TaxFlow shows:

  • Which exchange and layout it recognised. If it could not tell, choose the exchange from the list or use the column mapper.
  • A wallet name. It defaults to the exchange name. Keep one wallet name per account, and use the same name for every file from that account (for example both CoinSpot files under "CoinSpot").
  • Export time zone, if the file needs one. Pick the zone you downloaded it in, not where you live now if you have moved.

Select Add file (or Add selected files). Each file then shows how many rows became transactions, transfers, ignored rows and errors.

3. Review (5 minutes or more)

Open Review. This is the part that matters. Common items:

  • Unmatched transfers. A withdrawal from one exchange with no matching deposit elsewhere. Usually you moved coins to a wallet you have not imported: choose "My own wallet I didn't import" and name it.
  • Missing AUD values. Crypto-to-crypto swaps where the export has no AUD price. Enter the value, or turn on online price lookup and accept a suggestion.
  • Sales with no purchase on file. You sold more than TaxFlow can see you bought. Import the missing history, or add the purchase by hand.

Every fix shows how it changed your figures. See How review flags work.

4. Read the myTax guide (1 minute)

Open Reports and pick the tax year at the top. The myTax guide lists, in order, what to answer and what to type: whole dollars for myTax, with the exact cents beside them. Check the Checks panel is green and read the Assumptions: parcel method, time zone, airdrop treatment.

Then follow myTax item 18, step by step to enter the figures, and Other income for any staking or rewards.

5. Keep a copy

Export your project from Settings and keep it somewhere private. Download the tax agent pack too: it is your record of how every figure was worked out, which the ATO expects you to keep for five years.

Note

TaxFlow gives general information, not tax advice. If something in Review does not make sense, or the result surprises you, check it with a registered tax agent before you lodge.
