Export your OKX history

Download OKX trading history and funding history as CSV, note the time zone you choose at export, and add both to TaxFlow.

Updated 17 October 2026Built-in reader

Follow along in the app. Your files stay on your device.

Open the app

OKX splits your history between Trading history (your spot trades) and Funding history (deposits, withdrawals and transfers). TaxFlow reads both. You need both: without funding history, coins you deposited look like they came from nowhere, and coins you withdrew look like they vanished.

How to get them

  1. Sign in to OKX on the web.
  2. Go to Assets, then Order center.
  3. Open Trading history and select Download.
  4. Choose all instruments, the full date range, your accounts, and CSV.
  5. Set the time zone to UTC if you can, and note it. OKX lets you choose the time zone for the export and does not write it in the file. UTC is the simplest to tell TaxFlow afterwards.
  6. Repeat from Funding history, with Download.

OKX only shows 90 days on screen, but the download covers longer periods. Its trading history goes back to February 2021; older activity may need OKX support. The download is often a zip containing one CSV. You can drop the zip straight into TaxFlow.

Add them to TaxFlow

  1. Open Import and drop in both files. TaxFlow recognises the layouts trading and funding.
  2. Use the wallet name OKX for both.
  3. Set Export time zone to the zone you chose on OKX's download screen. The file is refused until you do. A fixed offset is not the same as a city with daylight saving: if you exported at a fixed UTC+10, choose Australia/Brisbane (UTC+10 all year), not Sydney or Melbourne, which move to UTC+11 in summer.
  4. Select Add selected files.

Note

some OKX files write times with a zone abbreviation like CST. Abbreviations are ambiguous worldwide (China, Cuba and US Central all use CST), so TaxFlow refuses them rather than guess. Download again with a numeric zone.

What TaxFlow does with it

Trading history. A spot trade is two rows with the same id: a Buy row for the asset you received and a Sell row for the asset you gave up. TaxFlow pairs them (falling back to Order id where ids do not match). The fee is in the received asset and is taken off it. Each row's balance change is checked against its direction.

  • Trade Type Transfer rows are ignored (your own balances).
  • Margin, perpetual swaps and futures are listed as unsupported.

Funding history. Deposits and withdrawals become one-sided transfers, paired with your other wallets. Each one's direction is checked against the before and after balances. Internal transfers are ignored; other types are listed as unsupported.

Older ledger files (time,type,size,balance,fee,currency) are recognised, but their vocabulary is not confirmed, so every row is listed as unsupported with a pointer to the column mapper.

Values you will need to add

Most OKX trades are against USDT or USDC, not AUD, so expect missing AUD values. Turn on Look up missing prices online in Settings and accept the suggestions in Review, or bring a price file. Stablecoins are crypto: a swap from BTC into USDT is a disposal of BTC.

Gotchas

  • OKX account statements (monthly or custom) are in UTC and are statements, not the trading history. Use the trading and funding history downloads.
  • Earn, staking and other products may not appear in trading history. Compare Holdings with OKX's balances (Holdings shows each wallet separately when Parcels is set to "A pool per wallet" in Settings) and add missing rewards by hand in Transactions.
  • P2P trades for AUD appear in funding history. If one shows as unsupported, add it by hand as a buy or sell.
