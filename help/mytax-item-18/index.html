myTax item 18, step by step (labels H, A and V)

Enter your crypto capital gains in myTax: the CGT event question, labels H, A and V, the CGT schedule over $10,000, and how TaxFlow gets each figure.

Crypto you sell, swap, spend or give away is a CGT asset, and the result goes in the Capital gains section of your return (item 18 on the paper and agent return). myTax pre-fills only an indicator that you may have had a crypto CGT event; it does not pre-fill amounts. The amounts come from you, and this is what the myTax guide on Reports prepares.

Note

TaxFlow gives general information and calculations, not tax advice. It is not a registered tax agent. If your situation is unusual, have a registered tax agent check these figures.

Before you start

  • Pick the right tax year at the top of the app.
  • Check Review is clear for that year. If not, the guide says "Finish Review first" and the YEAR_COMPLETE check is red. Figures with open items use placeholders.
  • Read the Assumptions on Reports: parcel method, taxpayer type, time zone, airdrop treatment and carried-in losses. Change them in Settings if they are wrong.

Step 1: open Capital gains or losses

In myTax: Income, then Australian income or losses from investments or property, then Capital gains. You may need to tick the capital gains option when you first prepare the return.

Step 2: answer the questions

  • Did you have a CGT event during the year? Yes, if you disposed of any crypto in the year: a sale, a swap, spending it, gifting it, or paying a network fee in crypto. TaxFlow shows the answer.
  • Have you applied an exemption, rollover or additional discount? For crypto, normally No. The 50% CGT discount is not an "additional discount" for this question. The personal use asset exemption is applied inside TaxFlow's figures where you marked it; ask your agent if you think a code should be entered.

Step 3: enter the three amounts

myTax field Label What it is TaxFlow source
Total current year capital gains H All capital gains made in the year, before losses and before the discount Sum of every gain (collectable gains after collectable losses)
Net capital gain A What is taxed: gains less current and prior losses, less the discount The method statement result
Net capital losses carried forward to later income years V Losses you could not use this year Unused current and prior losses

The guide shows each one in whole dollars (cents dropped, never rounded up), which is what myTax wants, with the exact cents beside it for your records. Each has a Copy button.

How A is worked out (the method statement)

TaxFlow follows the ATO's order:

  1. Add up gains. Gains on parcels held for at least 12 months are discount gains; the rest are non-discount gains. Their total is H.
  2. Take off this year's capital losses, non-discount gains first, then discount gains.
  3. Take off losses carried in from earlier years, in the same order.
  4. Halve what is left of the discount gains (one third off for an SMSF, nothing for a company).
  5. What remains is A. Unused losses are V.

A worked example for an individual: a $6,000 gain on BTC held two years, a $2,000 gain on ETH held five months, a $1,500 loss on SOL, and $1,000 of losses carried in.

  • H = $6,000 + $2,000 = $8,000
  • The $1,500 loss comes off the $2,000 non-discount gain, leaving $500.
  • $500 of the $1,000 carried-in loss clears that; the other $500 comes off the discount gain, leaving $5,500.
  • Discount: half of $5,500 = $2,750.
  • A = $5,500 − $2,750 = $2,750; V = $0.

Because losses go against non-discount gains first, they save the most tax.

Step 4: the CGT schedule, if needed

If your total current year capital gains or your total capital losses are more than $10,000, myTax asks for the CGT schedule. TaxFlow shows a panel with each schedule field already worked out. Crypto goes under Other CGT assets and any other CGT events; collectables have their own rows. The schedule's totals must agree with H, A and V, and TaxFlow checks that (SCHEDULE_MATCHES_ITEM_18).

Checks TaxFlow runs

  • INCDTLS.000423: net capital gain must be within $5 of gains less losses applied less the discount. This is a validation rule from the ATO's specifications for return software, so a figure that fails it would be questioned.
  • SCHEDULE_MATCHES_ITEM_18: the schedule agrees with H, A and V.
  • YEAR_COMPLETE: no open Review items in the year.

Also on your return

  • Staking, interest and other crypto income go in Other income, not here. See Other income.
  • Losses you carried in come from last year's label V: see Carried-forward losses.
  • If you trade crypto as a business, your figures go in the business schedule instead, and H, A and V are zero. See Trader or investor.
