Export your Bybit history

Bybit has no built-in importer yet. Export the Transaction Log and spot trade history, then map them with the column mapper.

TaxFlow has no built-in Bybit importer yet. Bybit's current spot and Unified Trading Account export headers have not been confirmed with a real file, and TaxFlow will not ship a layout it has not seen. You can still import Bybit with the column mapper, and save the mapping so next year takes a minute.

What to download

Export Where Notes
Transaction Log Assets, Unified Trading Account, History Records, Transaction Log, Export Every balance change. Up to 10,000 rows per export, a few exports a day.
Spot order and trade history Orders, Unified Trading Order, then the history tab, Export Six months per export, within the last two years.
Deposits and withdrawals Assets, then the deposit and withdrawal history Coins in and out

Choose CSV. Bybit can take from hours to a few days to prepare an export, and download links last about seven days. These paths come from Bybit's public help pages and have not been checked against a live account.

Note

Bybit only keeps two years of spot trade history for self-export. If you traded on Bybit before that, ask Bybit support for the older history, and look for older exports you saved.

Which file to map

For spot trading, map the spot trade history (one row per fill). Then map the deposits and withdrawals so TaxFlow can pair them with your other wallets. Use the Transaction Log for income such as Earn rewards, or as a cross-check. Do not map the same trades from two files, or they will be counted twice.

Map the trade history

  1. Open Import, drop the CSV in, and choose Map columns.
  2. Row shape: Trade fill (a pair such as BTCUSDT, a side, a quantity and a price or total).
  3. Columns: date, pair, side, filled quantity, and the filled value (quote total) or price. Map the fee and fee asset. If the pair has no separator, also map the base and quote asset columns if Bybit provides them.
  4. Transaction types: map buy and sell values to trade.
  5. Dates: Bybit usually states UTC in the column name. Choose UTC as the mapping time zone unless your file says otherwise, and tick the confirmation.
  6. Fees: for a spot fill, Bybit usually takes the fee from what you received. Check one trade against Bybit's screen and choose the matching fee mode.
  7. Dry run: check balances. Negative balances usually mean missing deposits or an older period you have not exported.
  8. Name it "Bybit spot" and save.

Map deposits and withdrawals

Use Ledger or Sent and received shape, map the type values to deposit and withdrawal (and internal transfers between Bybit accounts to ignore), and use the same wallet name, Bybit.

What to expect afterwards

  • Missing AUD values. Bybit trades are mostly against USDT. Each one needs an AUD value; turn on online price lookup in Settings and accept suggestions in Review.
  • Derivatives. Futures, perpetuals and options are outside TaxFlow's scope. Leave those rows unmapped (they are listed as unsupported) and give your agent Bybit's derivatives records separately.
  • GST. If you are registered for GST, confirm Bybit's location for your wallet in Settings. See BAS and GST for crypto.

If you can, send us the header row only (no transaction rows) of each Bybit file through the contact page. A confirmed header lets us add a built-in importer.
