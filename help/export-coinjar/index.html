Export your CoinJar history

Request CoinJar's Purchases and Sales report, name its date column (or use the mapper preset), and handle deposits and withdrawals.

Written from public documentation. These steps have not yet been checked against a live account, so a menu name may differ. If something has moved, tell us.

CoinJar documents the fields in its reports but not the exact header row, and does not name the date column. TaxFlow's CoinJar support is therefore experimental: it reads the report by CoinJar's documented column names, and asks you which column holds the date. If your file does not match, use the column mapper with its CoinJar preset.

What to request

CoinJar's main app has three reports that matter for tax:

Report What it holds TaxFlow
Purchases and Sales (CSV) Buys, sells and converts, with source, destination and fee Read (experimental)
Deposits and Withdrawals Coins and AUD in and out Not read automatically: use the mapper
Full account statement Everything, as a statement Not read

CoinJar Exchange (the separate trading product) has its own reports (Account Statement, Fill History and others). TaxFlow does not read those yet; use the mapper.

How to get them

  1. In CoinJar on the web, open More, then Reports. In the app: More, General, then Reports and statements.
  2. Request the Purchases and Sales report for your whole history.
  3. Request the Deposits and Withdrawals report too.
  4. CoinJar emails the reports, sometimes up to an hour later. Save the CSV attachments.

Add the Purchases and Sales report

  1. Open Import and drop the CSV in. Choose CoinJar as the exchange if it was not detected.
  2. TaxFlow fills in Date column name with its best guess (the first header containing "date" or "time"). Check it matches the date column in your file exactly.
  3. If the dates have no time zone (no Z or +10:00 at the end), set Export time zone to the zone you downloaded in.
  4. Keep the wallet name CoinJar and select Add file.

If TaxFlow says a documented column is missing, CoinJar's real header differs from its documentation. Select Map columns instead and start from the CoinJar preset (marked [U] for unconfirmed).

What TaxFlow does with it

  • Buy and Sell have an AUD side, which sets the cost or proceeds.
  • Convert (crypto to crypto) has no AUD value in the documented fields, so each convert is flagged in Review for a market value. Turn on online price lookup in Settings to get suggestions.
  • Fees. CoinJar documents the destination amount as gross, before fees. TaxFlow takes a fee in the destination currency off it. A fee in any other currency contradicts that documentation, so the row is listed as unsupported rather than guessed.
  • Other types are listed as unsupported for you to classify.

Deposits and withdrawals

Without the Deposits and Withdrawals report, coins you moved out of CoinJar look like they vanished, and coins you moved in look like they came from nowhere (you will see "Sales with no purchase on file"). Map the report with the column mapper: usually a ledger or sent/received shape, with deposits and withdrawals mapped as such. AUD movements have no tax effect.

Gotchas

  • Large files. CoinJar warns that reports over a million rows will not open in Excel. TaxFlow does not need Excel: drop the CSV straight in, and do not open and re-save it first (Excel can change dates and long numbers).
  • Help us confirm the layout. If you can, send us the header row only (the first line, no transactions) through the contact page. One real header lets us replace the experimental reader with a confirmed one.
