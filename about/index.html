About

Crypto tax should not need your data on someone else’s server.

TaxFlow is an independent project that works out Australian crypto tax entirely in the browser. It is not affiliated with the ATO or with any exchange.

Why

Why it exists.

To work out crypto tax, a service needs your entire trading history: every buy, sell, transfer and wallet. Most services ask you to upload that history or connect your exchange accounts to their servers, and keep it there.

Browsers are now fast enough to do the whole calculation on your own device. TaxFlow does exactly that, so the most sensitive financial records are processed locally. Optional online connections send the disclosed requests and responses to providers and, where shown, a hosted relay.

It is also built only for Australia. Instead of adapting a global product to the ATO, it starts from the Australian rules: the CGT discount and the order losses come off, myTax labels, SMSF and company treatment, trading stock, residency changes, Division 775 and the draft airdrop ruling.

How it is built

Rules first, then code, then proof.

Each tax rule is written down in a specification with its legal source before any code is written. Then it gets test cases, worked out by hand, that the engine must reproduce to the cent.

215Australian test cases, each worked out by hand from the law and ATO guidance
4parcel methods compared on every return: FIFO, LIFO, HIFO and LTFO
10exchange and wallet layouts read without any setup
LocalImported files and calculations are processed locally. Optional connections send disclosed requests and responses through providers and a hosted relay.

Exact arithmetic

Money and coin quantities use decimal arithmetic from start to finish, so there is no floating-point drift in a cost base.

No silent guesses

A missing price is flagged for review, never treated as zero. A file with no time zone is asked about, never assumed.

Independent review

Changes to the tax engine are reviewed against the specification and the hand-worked cases before they are accepted.

What it is not

Honest limits.

  • Not a tax agent. TaxFlow gives general information and calculations. It does not give advice about your circumstances.
  • Not a lodgement service. You type the figures into myTax, or your agent uses the pack.
  • Not externally audited yet. The test cases are thorough, but no accounting firm has signed off on the engine.
  • Not finished. TaxFlow is in beta. Read the changelog to see what has changed.
