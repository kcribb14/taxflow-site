General information only

Everything TaxFlow shows, including figures, reports, estimates, guides and explanations, is general information and calculation based on the data you import and the choices you make. It does not take your full circumstances into account and is not tax, financial or legal advice.

Not a registered tax agent

TaxFlow is not a registered tax agent or BAS agent under the Tax Agent Services Act 2009, and it does not provide tax agent services. If you need advice about your situation, or want someone to prepare and lodge your return, use a registered tax agent. You can check an agent’s registration on the Tax Practitioners Board register.

Figures depend on your data

The results can only be as good as the files you import. Missing transactions, wrong prices, unmatched transfers or a wrong choice in Settings will produce wrong figures. Review the items TaxFlow flags, and check the figures against your own records before you rely on them.

Tax law and ATO guidance change

TaxFlow follows the law and ATO guidance as understood when each part was written, including draft guidance such as TR 2026/D1 on airdrops, which may change when finalised. Rules, rates and the ATO’s views change over time, and TaxFlow may not reflect a change straight away.

Estimates are estimates

The tax estimate leaves out some items, such as the Medicare levy surcharge, study loan repayments and most offsets, and says so on screen. Treat it as a guide to the size of a bill or refund, not as the amount the ATO will assess.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the information in your tax return. Check your figures with a registered tax agent before you lodge.
