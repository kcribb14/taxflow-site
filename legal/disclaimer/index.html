General information only

Everything TaxFlow shows, including figures, reports, estimates, guides and explanations, is general information and calculation based on the records you add or connect and the choices you make. It does not take your full circumstances into account and is not tax, financial or legal advice.

Not a registered tax agent

TaxFlow is not a registered tax agent or BAS agent under the Tax Agent Services Act 2009, and it does not provide tax agent services. If you need advice about your situation, or want someone to prepare and lodge your return, use a registered tax agent. You can check an agent’s registration on the Tax Practitioners Board register (opens in a new tab).

Figures depend on your data

Results depend on the records you add or connect, the information each source makes available and the choices you make. A connection may not include every kind of activity. Missing transactions, wrong prices, unmatched transfers or a wrong choice in Settings can produce wrong figures. Review the items TaxFlow flags, and check the figures against your own records before you rely on them.

Tax law and ATO guidance change

TaxFlow follows the law and ATO guidance as understood when each part was written, including draft guidance such as TR 2026/D1 on airdrops, which may change when finalised. Rules, rates and the ATO’s views change over time, and TaxFlow may not reflect a change straight away.

Estimates are estimates

Coverage depends on the estimate path and income year. The estimate screen excludes the Medicare levy surcharge and most offsets, and includes HELP only within its checked 2025–26 scope. The guide also estimates surcharge, seniors offsets and study loans with stated income-test limits. Unclassified 2026–27 work deductions block a supported result; the standard deduction is included only with checked labour and expense facts. Each result names its rule version, legal status and check date on screen. Treat it as a guide to the size of a bill or refund, not as the amount the ATO will assess.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the information in your tax return. Check your figures with a registered tax agent before you lodge.
