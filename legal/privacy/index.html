Privacy policy

The short version: TaxFlow does not collect your data. It has nowhere to put it.

Last updated 17 October 2026

What TaxFlow collects

Nothing. TaxFlow has no accounts, no server-side database, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The files you import, the fixes you make and your settings are processed and stored only in your browser on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Your project (imported files, fixes, manual transactions and settings) in the browser’s IndexedDB storage.
  • Prices you accepted from an online lookup, in a separate IndexedDB store.
  • Your light or dark theme choice for this website, in the browser’s local storage.

You can delete your project at any time in the app’s Settings, and remove everything by clearing this site’s data in your browser. The security page explains each item.

The website host

This website and the app are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard request information when your browser loads a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in its logs. That is handled under GitHub’s own privacy statement. GitHub never receives your tax data, because the app never sends it.

Optional price lookups

Online price lookup is off by default. If you turn it on, your browser contacts the named price services directly to fill in missing Australian dollar prices: BTC Markets, Kraken, CoinGecko (only if you add your own key), Coinbase and Binance for coin prices, and Frankfurter for currency exchange rates.

Each request contains a coin symbol (such as BTC), a currency and a date or date range. It never contains amounts, balances, wallet names, addresses or your files. Those services receive the request information any website does, such as your IP address, and handle it under their own policies. TaxFlow does not receive a copy.

Files you export

Reports, agent packs and project backups are created in your browser and saved where you choose. A project backup contains the raw contents of your imported files, fixes and settings, so keep it private. New backups and reports omit provider API keys. Readable exports are not encrypted by browser protection. Private backup has its own password, which TaxFlow cannot reset.

Questions

Ask through a public GitHub issue. Do not include personal or financial details. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
