How it works

Three steps from exchange files to return-ready figures.

The same order as the app: import, review, report. Each screen says what it needs from you and why.

  1. 01

    Import the files your exchanges give you

    Drop in CSV, Excel or zip exports. TaxFlow recognises ten layouts on sight and shows exactly which rows it read, which it set aside and why.

    • CoinSpot, Swyftx, BTC Markets, Kraken, Binance, Coinbase and more
    • A seven-step column mapper for everything else, saved for next time
    • Times without a zone are asked about, never guessed
  2. 02

    Fix what is flagged, in plain English

    Anything that would make a figure wrong is listed with a fix: a missing price, a purchase with no history, a transfer with no other side. The figures update as you go.

    • Missing AUD values and missing cost bases
    • Unmatched transfers between your wallets
    • Every fix is stored in your project and can be undone
  3. 03

    Take the numbers to myTax or your agent

    Reports give the whole dollars to type into each myTax label, the CGT schedule, other income and an agent pack with every disposal behind them.

    • FIFO, LIFO, HIFO and LTFO compared side by side
    • Assumptions and open review items shown on the report
    • CSV exports, plus BGL and Class files for SMSFs

Privacy

Your history stays on your device.

TaxFlow is a web page that does all its work in the tab you open. There is no TaxFlow server, account or database holding your trades, so there is nothing of yours for us to lose, sell or hand over.

Questions

Asked before you start.

More in the help centre, with 46 guides.

Is TaxFlow really free?

Yes. Everything is free while TaxFlow is in beta. There is no account, no card and no limit on the number of transactions. Pricing after the beta has not been set.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser on this device, in its local database (IndexedDB). Your files are read in the page and the calculation runs in a background worker in the same tab. There is no TaxFlow server to send them to.

Does anything leave my device?

Not unless you turn on online price lookup. Then TaxFlow asks public price services for the AUD price of a coin on a date. It sends coin symbols and dates, never amounts, balances or wallets.

Which exchanges can I import?

CoinSpot, Swyftx, BTC Markets, Kraken, Binance, Coinbase, the Crypto.com App, OKX and KuCoin have built-in readers, as does the Koinly universal template for wallets. Independent Reserve and CoinJar have mapper presets, and the column mapper handles any other CSV, Excel or zip file.

Can I lodge my return with TaxFlow?

No. TaxFlow does not lodge anything. It gives you the figures to type into myTax, or a pack of working to hand to your tax agent.

Is this tax advice?

No. TaxFlow gives general information and calculations from the data you import. It is not a registered tax agent. Check your figures with one before you lodge, especially if your situation is unusual.

Which parcel method should I use?

TaxFlow works out FIFO, LIFO, HIFO and LTFO and shows the result of each. The ATO accepts a method other than first in, first out when your records identify the parcels you sold, so choose one your records support.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your project is deleted with it, because the browser holds the only copy. Export your project as a backup file from Settings, and keep your original exchange files.

How do I know the maths is right?

The tax engine is checked against 215 Australian test cases, each worked by hand from the legislation and ATO guidance, before any change is accepted. Figures still depend on the files you import, so review the flagged items.
