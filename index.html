How your data is handled

You do not need to create an account or hand your tax files to a TaxFlow server to get started.

Your files are processed locally and your project is saved in this browser. Keep a backup: clearing browser data can remove it.

Imported files and calculations are processed locally. Optional online connections send the disclosed requests and responses to providers and, where shown, a hosted relay. Review connection privacy details.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Visit the help centre
Can I use it without crypto?

Yes. You can enter income totals or keep wage, Australian bank interest and deduction records. Optional income CSVs and supported one-page payslip PDFs create drafts for you to check. A single payslip does not establish a full year of income.

Does TaxFlow lodge my return?

No. It prepares working figures and exports for the supported parts of your tax. You still check coverage and lodge through myTax or a registered tax agent. It does not complete every part of a tax return or provide personal tax advice.

What does it cost?

TaxFlow is free while in beta. No account or card is required. Pricing after beta has not been set.

How do I keep my work safe?

Your work is saved in this browser. Download a project backup from Settings and keep your original files. Clearing browser data can remove your saved work. Optional browser protection and encrypted backups are available in Settings.
