Changelog

What has changed.

Each release of TaxFlow, newest first, in plain words.

v317 October 2026

A proper home: website and help centre

TaxFlow gets a website that explains what it does and how your data is kept, and a help centre for the questions people ask first.

  • A product website: features, supported exchanges, security, pricing and the legal pages.
  • A help centre with guides grouped by topic, a search box, and a page per exchange explaining which file to download.
  • A clear statement of every source TaxFlow reads, and how well: built-in reader, mapper preset or column mapper.
v217 October 2026

Businesses, SMSFs and many more exchanges

TaxFlow grows from capital gains for individuals into BAS, estimates and fund exports, and reads files from the big global exchanges.

  • BAS and GST working, quarterly or monthly, and PAYG instalment working for crypto businesses.
  • A tax estimate for 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27, stating what it leaves out.
  • SMSF exports: a BGL Simple Fund 360 File Import XML built from BGL’s official template, and BGL and Class worksheets.
  • New built-in readers for Binance, Coinbase, the Crypto.com App, OKX and KuCoin, plus Excel and zip files.
  • A seven-step column mapper with saved profiles, and presets for Independent Reserve and CoinJar.
  • Optional online price lookup that shows the source and date of every price and waits for you to accept it.
  • Traders, foreign and temporary residency, collectables, personal use assets and Division 775 foreign currency.
  • The tax engine is now checked against 215 hand-worked Australian cases.
v117 October 2026

The first working version

A new Australian tax engine and a web app around it, running entirely in the browser.

  • A capital gains engine for Australia with exact decimal arithmetic, the 12-month discount and the correct order for applying losses.
  • Readers for CoinSpot, Swyftx, BTC Markets, Kraken and the Koinly universal template.
  • Automatic matching of transfers between your own wallets, with anything uncertain sent to review.
  • Parcel methods FIFO, LIFO, HIFO and LTFO (lowest tax first out).
  • Airdrops treated as the draft ruling TR 2026/D1 describes.
  • Seven screens: Overview, Import, Review, Transactions, Reports, Holdings and Settings, with the myTax figures and an agent pack.
  • A sample portfolio to try it without your own files.
